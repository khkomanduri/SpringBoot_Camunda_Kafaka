Alphabets, Genders & Persons

There are 26 letters in English, 4 Extra letters in German.

A – Z, Ä, Ö, Ü, ß (eszett).

G- Gender – 1.Masculine 2.Neutral 3. Feminine

N- Number – 1. Singular 2.Plural

P- Person – 1st Person, 2nd Person, 3rd Person.

1st – One who speaks (For / About self)

2nd - With/To whom one Speaks.

3rd - About whom / What / Which one speaks.
